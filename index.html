Match all 3 reels for the jackpot multiplier shown. Match exactly 2 reels to get your bet back.

How to Play

  1. Enter a whole-dollar bet between $5 and $50 (or use the Min/Max buttons).
  2. Click SPIN REELS (or pull the lever) — the bet is deducted and all 3 reels spin.
  3. Land 3 matching symbols for a jackpot payout, 2 matching symbols to get your bet back, or no match to lose your bet.
  4. Your balance updates automatically. Bet again and keep playing!
